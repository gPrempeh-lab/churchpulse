# ChurchPulse

> Measure attendance. Understand growth. Strengthen ministry.

ChurchPulse is a church attendance and analytics platform for tracking attendance, trends, visitor engagement, and ministry growth.

Designed for churches of all sizes, ChurchPulse helps church leaders capture attendance data, monitor congregation growth, identify engagement patterns, and make informed ministry and planning decisions through meaningful insights and reporting.

## Features

### Attendance Management
- Record attendance for church services
- Track adults, youths, and children
- Monitor first-time and returning visitors
- View attendance history

### Analytics & Reporting
- Attendance trends
- Monthly and yearly reports
- Growth analysis
- Visitor insights
- Dashboard statistics

### Future Features
- Member management
- Volunteer management
- Event tracking
- Role-based access
- Report exports (CSV/PDF)
- Mobile-friendly experience

## Vision

To empower churches with reliable attendance tracking and actionable insights that support strategic planning, congregational growth, and effective ministry leadership.
